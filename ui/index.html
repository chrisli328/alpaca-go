Home Works

How to get here
The game opens here. Home tab.
What it does
The ranch with the herd wandering. Every building is a door: barn (Breed), shearing shed, silo, training ring, Carrot Patch, feeding trough. Grey buildings with a padlock open the locked screen. Stickers on the left: CHAMPIONS CUP (Race tab), BREED NOW (Breed), WOOL ORDERS (Rosa's Wool Orders). The goal bar on top shows the next career race and whether the alpaca is ready.
Note
The three buttons on the right (mail, missions, gift) shake and say "Coming soon": their pop-ups exist (see Pop-ups) but nothing opens them yet. The same in the HTML prototype. The green + by the coins would open the Shop, which is shut.

Headline Cup Works

How to get here
Race tab, then the Headline Cup row. Or Go on Rosa's letter.
What it does
Today's one featured race and how well each alpaca suits it. Three entries a day for the whole ranch. The day's first top 3 pays 1,000 coins and a Golden Carrot, any other top 3 pays 200. It never changes a career.

Championship Numbers are placeholders

How to get here
Race tab, then the Championship row, once the ranch reaches Stable Rank E2. (This picture was opened directly.)
What it does
The monthly championship: the conditions come out on the 1st, the player prepares an alpaca and picks it as the entry, three qualifiers run by themselves from the 25th, and the final on the last day is watched as a real race. Rewards, Past winners and Help open pop-ups.
Note
Every course, number, prize and past winner is a placeholder. Other ranches are made up: there is no server yet.

Care Numbers are placeholders

How to get here
Alpacas, then CARE. Or tap the mood word on an alpaca's card.
What it does
Each day an alpaca can be petted three times, brushed once and given one treat. Care gives hearts, which build the bond (it never drops), and lifts the mood (which drops by itself). Both make training pay more; mood also nudges a race.
Note
Every number is a placeholder.

A race 7

One race from the start gate to the result.

These come from the race capture (Alpaca.EditorTools.CaptureRun.Stills), which starts a stand-alone cup straight away. In play a race starts from ENTER on the Career or the Headline Cup, or the Championship final. The race screens are the same; only the result differs: a career or Headline race has no Race Again, and its other button says Career, Headline or Champs instead of Ranch. The head-gear prize on this result is from the stand-alone cup.
